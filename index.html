About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Coastal Engineering Laboratory, Department of Civil Engineering, The University of Tokyo, supervised by Prof. Yoshimitsu Tajima, fully funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). My current doctoral research focuses on meshfree SPH (Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics) modeling of shallow-water wave evolution, wave breaking mechanisms, and wave-induced mass transport in surf zones and complex reef topographies.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I obtained my M.Eng. in Civil Engineering from Shanghai University (supervised by Prof. Zili Dai), where I developed a novel fully-Lagrangian coupled PD-SPH (Peridynamics - Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics) solver to analyze dynamic fluid-structure interaction and structural fracture under extreme hydrodynamic loads. I hold a B.Eng. in Software Engineering from East China Jiaotong University, combining strong computational skills with engineering domain expertise.
