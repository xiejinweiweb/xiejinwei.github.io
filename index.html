About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Tokyo. My research primarily focuses on wave-coastal structure interactions, wave breaking mechanisms, and hydrodynamic responses. Previously, my master's research centered on structural fracture simulations under fluid-structure interactions (FSI) using hydrodynamic impact models. I possess a strong foundation in both civil engineering and software development, and I am highly motivated by bridging field investigations, laboratory experiments, and numerical modeling.
